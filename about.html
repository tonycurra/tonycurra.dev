Embedded Systems & Edge AI Engineer

Who I am

I build a device until someone can use it. The parts, the software inside, the sensors, the battery. When it has to recognise something, it does that on the device, not on a server. I trained as a mechanical engineer, so the case is part of the same job.

Since 2024 I have done this remotely, for medical devices, environmental sensors, and farm robots.

The work I want next is the thinking on the device. It keeps working with no internet, and the battery still lasts.

Olivabot B.V. is my company in Amsterdam, and the lab where I worked this out. Remote work is invoiced from there. mail@tonycurra.dev
