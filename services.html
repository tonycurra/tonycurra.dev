The model, on your device

The model works on a computer. The device cannot run it. I put it on the device, fast enough, without emptying the battery.

On a rehabilitation glove, the sensor is the size of a ring, and the device itself recognises how the hand moves.

A two-week answer

One price, about two weeks. I tell you whether the model can run on your device, how fast, what it does to the battery, and what it would take to finish. Then you decide.

The software inside the device

It reads the sensors, talks to a phone or a network, and lasts on a battery.

A portable detector the UK police used in the field. More than 8 hours on one charge.

From the idea to something you can try

You have the idea, and nobody to build the device. I take it to something a person can hold and try: the parts, the case, the battery, the software, and a page where the numbers show up.
